# XUXU

Aaron's DAW, built for recording.

## Download

Get the one for your computer. You install it once and it updates itself from then on.

### Windows

**[Download XUXU 0.3.12 for Windows](https://github.com/aarontrooperprod/xuxu-actualizaciones/raw/main/XUXU.zip)** (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, 4.4 MB)

1. Unzip it into a folder of yours (for example `Documents\XUXU`), not in Program Files.
2. Open `XUXU.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** and then **Run anyway**.
3. From then on XUXU updates itself: when you open it, it tells you if there's a new version and asks. There's also **Options → Check for Updates…**.

## What's new in 0.3.12

## XUXU 0.3.12 🎛️
**XUXU plug-ins** (Library → XUXU folder, at the top)
- **DeEsser:** tames harsh "s" sounds in vocals. Two knobs: **Find** and **Remove**. "Advanced" has four modes and **Split into two tracks**.
- **MultiMeter**, like Logic's: spectrum, goniometer, peaks and LUFS.

**MicroMeters** (View → MicroMeters)
- MiniMeters-style meters inside XUXU: waveform, stereo, spectrum, LUFS, VU and more. Dock them top, bottom or on the sides, or pop them into their own window.

**Mixer**
- **Stereo you can see:** the Master and stereo tracks show two lines in the meter, and stereo clips show two waveforms.
- **Solo** lights up blue and no longer gets stuck.
- The metronome turns yellow when on. Stop lights up while stopped.

**Arrangement**
- Importing audio over a clip trims it, like Ableton. Ctrl+Z brings it back.

**VST3 plug-ins**
- **Your own folder:** Options → Plug-in Folders… → Add Folder…
- **Full Rescan** retries the plug-ins that failed to load.

**Other**
- Default track names follow the language (Pista 2 → Track 2).
- Dark title bar on Windows 10 too.
