# XUXU

Aaron's DAW, built for recording.

## Download

Get the one for your computer. You install it once and it updates itself from then on.

### Windows

**[Download XUXU 0.3.26 for Windows](https://github.com/aarontrooperprod/xuxu-actualizaciones/raw/main/XUXU.zip)** (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, 4.7 MB)

1. Unzip it into a folder of yours (for example `Documents\XUXU`), not in Program Files.
2. Open `XUXU.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** and then **Run anyway**.
3. From then on XUXU updates itself: when you open it, it tells you if there's a new version and asks. There's also **Options → Check for Updates…**.

## What's new in 0.3.26

## XUXU 0.3.26 🎛️
**No more track limit**
- XUXU used to stop at 32 tracks (about 40 counting groups). Now it takes up to 1024.
- **Bounce to New Track** no longer mutes the original without making the new track. If a track can't be made, nothing changes and XUXU tells you.
- **Bounce Track in Place** works on all your selected tracks at once. One Ctrl+Z undoes it all.

**CPU meter, like Live**
- In the top bar: **CPU** with a bar and its %. Green, yellow over 70 %, red over 90 %. A red dot means the audio dropped out.
- Click it for a panel: each core, the heaviest tracks and their plug-in, every dropout with its bar (click to jump there) and the RAM.

**Smoother**
- Pressing Play no longer drops out at the start with heavy plug-ins like Vovious.
- Zooming (Ctrl+wheel) is much smoother in big projects.

**Big projects open fast again**
- Projects with lots of VocAlign could get huge (one was 661 MB) and take minutes to open and save. Now they stay small. Open yours and save once to shrink it.

**DynAssist**
- Its window no longer comes up empty with several tracks. Like in Pro Tools, it shows the track you select, or the one whose window you open.
