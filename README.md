# XUXU

Aaron's DAW, built for recording.

## Download

Get the one for your computer. You install it once and it updates itself from then on.

### Windows

**[Download XUXU 0.3.23 for Windows](https://github.com/aarontrooperprod/xuxu-actualizaciones/raw/main/XUXU.zip)** (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, 4.6 MB)

1. Unzip it into a folder of yours (for example `Documents\XUXU`), not in Program Files.
2. Open `XUXU.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** and then **Run anyway**.
3. From then on XUXU updates itself: when you open it, it tells you if there's a new version and asks. There's also **Options → Check for Updates…**.

## What's new in 0.3.23

## XUXU 0.3.23 ⏱️
**Tempo travels with your files, like Logic**
- Exported WAV and MP3 now carry the project tempo (**Include Tempo** in Export, on by default).
- Drop a MIDI or a long stem with another tempo and XUXU asks **"Use 128 BPM?"**. Say yes and the project takes it, so nothing gets stretched. One Ctrl+Z undoes it all.
- Change your answer later in Preferences → Audio → Import.

**Importing audio is way faster**
- A 3-minute song at another sample rate went from ~8 s to under half a second.
- Dropping files from Explorer or Splice is faster too.

**Sidechain on XUXU's own plug-ins**
- The **Compressor** and the **DeEsser** can listen to another track, with the Sidechain bar on top of their window.
- DeEsser: the other track decides where the S's are, this one gets cleaned (like FabFilter Pro-DS).

**Instrument tracks**
- The first insert slot is only for the instrument, in the track's color. Effects go below it.

**Also**
- The playhead no longer shakes while it follows the song.
- MicroMeters: square corners, no gaps or lines between meters.
