# XUXU

Aaron's DAW, built for recording.

## Download

Get the one for your computer. You install it once and it updates itself from then on.

### Windows

**[Download XUXU 0.3.21 for Windows](https://github.com/aarontrooperprod/xuxu-actualizaciones/raw/main/XUXU.zip)** (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, 4.6 MB)

1. Unzip it into a folder of yours (for example `Documents\XUXU`), not in Program Files.
2. Open `XUXU.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** and then **Run anyway**.
3. From then on XUXU updates itself: when you open it, it tells you if there's a new version and asks. There's also **Options → Check for Updates…**.

## What's new in 0.3.21

## XUXU 0.3.21 🎙️
**VocAlign works with ARA, like in Pro Tools and Cubase**
- Put VocAlign on the guide track **and** on the track you want to align.
- Open either one, click the guide clip (by its name bar) and hit **Capture** under GUIDE.
- Click the clip to align and hit **Capture** under DUB. It aligns right away, no need to press play.
- You can also drag a time selection instead of clicking a clip.
- What you captured is saved with the project and comes back when you open it.
- Melodyne and Vovious work just like before.
